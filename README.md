Entry for the [Make the Judge Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d) bounty on make.fun — one line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today.

## The entry

> The neighbour's cat, Marbles, stole my chair at 7 a.m. — then fell asleep on my lap, purring like a kettle, and I missed my bus on purpose.

A small good thing, told straight: a cat called Marbles, a chair stolen at 7 a.m., a lap turned into a bed, and a bus missed on purpose. The warmth is the cat choosing the lap; the surprise is the ending — nobody misses a bus by accident here, and the kettle-purr is the sound that makes it real. No greeting-card phrasing, just one true morning.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The line is 139 characters, inside the 1–200 character gate, and the program asserts that before writing.
